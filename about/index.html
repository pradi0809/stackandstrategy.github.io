About the publication

Ideas with depth.
Perspectives with direction.

Stack & Strategy is an independent publication about the decisions shaping modern technology and the people responsible for making them.

THE EDITORIAL INTENT

Technology is a means. Outcomes are the point.

New tools and architectures arrive constantly. The harder work is understanding where they fit, what trade-offs they introduce, and how they change the way organizations build and operate technology.

Stack & Strategy explores that work through four connected lenses: AI engineering, enterprise architecture, engineering leadership, and technology strategy.

Practical, independent, considered.

The publication values clear reasoning over hype, useful detail over jargon, and thoughtful disagreement over easy answers. Articles draw on industry patterns, technical exploration, and lessons from the practice of building technology systems and teams.

Stack & Strategy is an editorial identity—not a vendor, consultancy, or product endorsement channel. Ideas are meant to stand on their own; the conversation continues in the discussion under each article.
